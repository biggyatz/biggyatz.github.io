What land in Kathmandu costs per aana: asking prices from 1,923 listings

Key findings from 1,923 property listings across 11 districts: what an aana costs, where, and what moves the price.

Biggyat Kumar Pandey
3 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: across 1,923 listings, land asked a median of about Rs 27 lakh per aana in Kathmandu, Rs 23 lakh in Bhaktapur and Rs 17 lakh in Lalitpur, with prime localities like Baluwatar reaching Rs 55–60 lakh.

Ask five people in Kathmandu what a plot is worth and you'll get five answers. I wanted numbers instead, so I analysed 1,923 public property listings from 99aana.com and Hamrobazaar across 11 districts and 111 localities, and built a free price estimator on top of the data.

These are asking prices, not final sale prices, and most listings date from 2021, with smaller samples up to 2026. Treat every figure as an upper reference point.

Key findings

  • Land in Kathmandu district asks about Rs 27 lakh per aana (median), Bhaktapur about Rs 23 lakh and Lalitpur about Rs 17 lakh.
  • Location dominates. Prime localities such as Baluwatar and Dhumbarahi asked Rs 55–60 lakh per aana, several times more than the valley's outer edges.
  • A typical valley house sits on about 3.5 aana and asks around Rs 2.4 crore. Houses ask roughly 2 to 4 times more per aana than empty plots, because the price includes the building.
  • Road access and storeys move the price. Wider road access and each additional storey raise asking prices noticeably, even within the same locality.
  • Outside the valley it's a different market: land in Chitwan or Rupandehi asks under a sixth of the Kathmandu figure.

Converting Nepali land units

  • Ropani system (hills, valley): 1 ropani = 16 aana = 5,476 sq ft. 1 aana = 4 paisa = 342.25 sq ft. 1 paisa = 4 dam.
  • Bigha system (Terai): 1 bigha = 20 kattha = 72,900 sq ft. 1 kattha = 20 dhur = 3,645 sq ft, or about 10.65 aana.

Frequently asked questions

How much is one aana of land in Kathmandu?

Across land listings in Kathmandu district, the median asking price was about Rs 27 lakh per aana. Prime localities such as Baluwatar and Dhumbarahi asked Rs 55 to 60 lakh. These are asking prices, which are usually above final sale prices.

How many square feet is one aana?

One aana is 342.25 square feet. There are 16 aana in a ropani (5,476 sq ft), and one aana is divided into 4 paisa.

How do you convert kattha to aana?

One kattha is 3,645 square feet, which is about 10.65 aana. One bigha is 20 kattha (72,900 sq ft).
