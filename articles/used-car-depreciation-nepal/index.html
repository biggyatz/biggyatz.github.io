How fast used cars lose value in Nepal

Key findings from 422 used-car listings compared with 2026 showroom prices: how much value cars keep with age, and what matters most.

Biggyat Kumar Pandey
3 November 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: compared with today's showroom price of the same model, used cars in Nepal ask about 74% at 4–5 years, 60% at 6–8 years, 47% at 9–12 years and 29% at 13+ years.

There's no Kelley Blue Book for Nepal, so used-car prices get set by word of mouth. I analysed 422 used-car listings from Hamrobazaar across 68 models and compared them with 2026 showroom prices, then built a free used-car value estimator from the data.

The typical car in the data is 10 years old, has done 61,500 km and asks Rs 18.6 lakh. Hyundai dominates the market, followed by Suzuki, Toyota, Nissan, Kia and Tata.

Key findings

  • Value retained, compared with today's new price of the same model: about 74% at 4–5 years, 60% at 6–8 years, 47% at 9–12 years and 29% at 13+ years.
  • Cars hold value well in Nepal compared with many countries, largely because import duties keep new-car prices high.
  • Some brands hold up better than others. Early signals suggest Nissan and Kia keep more of their value than the market's most common brands over the same ages.
  • Kilometres barely move the asking price. Age and model explain most of it.
  • Electric cars are still a small sample, but early data suggests they lose value slightly faster with age than petrol cars.

On listings it hadn't seen, the estimator's typical error is 7.7%. It also converts Bikram Sambat model years (a "2075 model" is about 2018 AD).

Frequently asked questions

How much value does a car lose per year in Nepal?

Compared with today's showroom price of the same model, used cars in Nepal ask about 74% at 4 to 5 years old, 60% at 6 to 8 years, 47% at 9 to 12 years, and 29% at 13 years or older.

How do I convert a Bikram Sambat car model year to AD?

Subtract about 57 years: a 2075 BS model is roughly 2018 AD. The Nepali year starts in mid-April, so the exact AD year can differ by one.
